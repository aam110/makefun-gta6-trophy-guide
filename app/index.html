The road to the platinum

Three stages, about 200 hours, one playthrough. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission replay — so save at the start of every chapter.

⚠ One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 may only be available during that chapter (GTABase, PowerPyx). Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a manual save from the chapter’s start. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

About this guide

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation text, as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed the 55-trophy set on 18 November 2026 (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden until earned). Every guide is written for this entry in its own words and names its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. Where no guide source has confirmed a method, the guide says so plainly instead of presenting a guess as fact.

Your progress lives only in this browser (localStorage) and in the export file you choose to save. The app loads no network resources and works with the network off. This is an unofficial fan-made guide; it uses no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or screenshots of the game.

Sources consulted: Exophase · PSNProfiles · GTABase · PowerPyx · Output Lag · PlanetGTA6 · GTA Vibes · GTA Zone
